I am a Ph.D. candidate in Finance and Economics at Warwick Business School.

My research links corporate finance and asset pricing through investor demand. Drawing on empirical industrial organization, I develop and estimate structural demand systems that measure how investors substitute across securities and how their demand moves asset prices. I show how investor demand can make both issuing and repurchasing shares costly, and study how public float shapes these costs and, through them, firms' financing, payout, and cash policies.
